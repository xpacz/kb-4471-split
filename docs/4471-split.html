The two problems

  1. Parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant's dates come out flipped, so 7 April arrives as 4 July. This is the problem #4471 describes, and the queued fix covers it.
  2. Form hint. Through booking-v3, people type what the hint under the date field tells them, month first, and the form reads it the tenant's way. A parser fix does not touch this. The fix is the hint text, or reading the field as the hint says.

Each problem has its own issue in xpacz/kb-4471-split.

A lead, not a problem

The desk's own notes (desk-notes.json) read some reports as a move by one day, not a swap. They are counted under the parser, where they are filed. Their words point at time zones: one meeting reached Lisbon on Tuesday and Toronto on Monday, one invite arrived before the booking existed, one tenant sees it only in June. That reading is mine, from a few reports. An engineer should reproduce it before it gets a ticket (see issue 3, closed).

Why one ticket held two

  • The desk merges on the subject line (decision 0001). Both problems arrive under the same few subjects, so the merge could not tell them apart.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser. That is a guess, and for the form-hint reports it is wrong about as often as it is right. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run.

Lessons

  • A ticket's count says how often people hit a problem, not how many problems there are. Check the cause before you merge, not only the subject.
  • Merge by cause (sameCause in causes.ts), not by subject.
